# ParrotReady 1.6.1

## Downloads

- **Windows:** download `ParrotReady-Setup-1.6.1-x64.exe` and run the installer.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel):** download `ParrotReady-1.6.1-universal.dmg`, open it, and drag ParrotReady into Applications. Quit the previous version before replacing it.

These community builds do not have a paid publisher certificate or Apple notarization. Windows may show an unknown-publisher prompt. macOS may require allowing ParrotReady in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after attempting to open it.

## What's new

- **Separate GPN DET Sample practice:** all 1,080 numbered samples across 14 sections, preserving the booklet's numbering and answer keys.
- Includes 80 original question photos, 100 original question recordings, three scored example recordings, and the complete reference PDF.
- **Natural American English voices:** 1,008 bundled Heart/Michael audio clips, available offline. Original booklet recordings retain their original speakers.
- **Better writing feedback:** improved examples develop a complete answer even when the submitted answer is short; grading still evaluates the original response.
- More reliable playback, replay limits, question transitions, voice selection, and failure recovery.
- Voice previews support immediate speed changes and preserve pitch. Use **Settings → Natural US voices → 1×** for the original pace.

GPN practice remains separate from the normal question banks and exams. Practice content and bundled audio work offline. AI grading requires an online provider configured on each user's computer; no developer account or API key is bundled.

## Verification

The update includes source-data checks, all 1,008 voice routes, playback recovery tests, and prior coverage of 1,160 GPN practice attempts. Windows and universal Mac installers are built on GitHub Actions. Automated audio checks verify sampled intelligibility, not subjective naturalness.
